# The Math Behind Spatial Pattern Formation

Spatial patterns are present throughout many areas of nature such as in animal patterns and vegetation growth. Turing pat- terns are one way of mathematically modeling spatial pattern formation, and the reaction-di!usion principles which define the model have been shown to be the likely biological cause of pattern formation in animals. Due to their direct tie to the natural phenomenon they represent, Turing patterns are a significant area of interest in mathematical biology. The pat- tern formation arises from complex interactions between two or more substances di!using into each other. With proper analysis, the dynamics can be made to generate an infinite number of varying patterns. This work studies the Schnaken- berg kinetics, known to generate spot patterns, and performs a linear stability analysis to determine the necessary condi- tions for generating Turing patterns. Parameters are chosen based on this analysis to simulate spot patterns similar to those seen on a cheetah.
